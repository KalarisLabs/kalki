# Code of Conduct

Kalki follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That document is the full text of our standards. It applies to every project
space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel the project uses.

In short: be respectful, argue about the work and not the person, and assume the
other contributor is acting in good faith. Harassment, personal attacks, and
publishing private information about another person are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Email **hello@kalarislabs.com** with `[CONDUCT]` in the subject. Reports are read
by the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) and kept confidential.
If your report concerns a maintainer, say so and the others will handle it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction,
warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. The response matches the impact.
